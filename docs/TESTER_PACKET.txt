PressTalk 0.1.7 — test build
============================

Hold the Fn key (bottom-left, the one with the globe), say a sentence, let go.
The text appears in whatever app you were typing in. Speech recognition runs on
your Mac — nothing is uploaded.

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or later.
Not compatible with Intel Macs.


1. INSTALL
----------
Unzip PressTalk-0.1.7-macos-arm64.zip and drag PressTalk.app into Applications.


2. THE FIRST LAUNCH WILL BE BLOCKED — this is expected
------------------------------------------------------
This build isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS
refuses it the first time. It is not broken and it is not a virus; it just
hasn't been through Apple's paperwork.

  Double-click PressTalk. You'll get "Apple could not verify PressTalk is free
  of malware". Click Done.

  Then: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway"
  next to PressTalk. Confirm.

  (On macOS 14 you can instead right-click the app > Open > Open.)

You only do this once.


3. FIRST RUN
------------
A setup window walks you through three macOS permissions, one at a time:
microphone, input monitoring (to notice the Fn key), accessibility (to paste).

Then it downloads the speech model, about 460 MB. Once. After that it works
offline — you can turn off Wi-Fi and it still works.

Setup finishes when you've successfully dictated once.

Note: this build doesn't add itself to login items, so after a restart you'll
need to open PressTalk again from Applications.


4. WHAT I'D LIKE YOU TO DO
--------------------------
Please actually use it for two days for real work. Emails, messages, notes,
Slack, whatever you'd normally type. That's the only way to find out if it's
good or just impressive for thirty seconds.

If you have twenty minutes and want to be more systematic: do about 20
dictations across two different apps and note how many didn't work.


5. WHAT I'D LIKE TO KNOW
------------------------
Short answers are fine. Blunt answers are better.

  1. Did you get to your first working dictation without asking me for help?
     Roughly how long did it take?

  2. Out of your dictations, how many failed — nothing appeared, or the text
     went somewhere wrong?

  3. How good was the text? Did you have to fix things, and what kind of things?

  4. How does it compare to just pressing the dictation key macOS already has?
     (If you don't know it: press the microphone key, or Fn twice.)

  5. Did you keep using it, or did you stop? Why?

  6. It'll be $20, one payment, no subscription, and every future update
     included. Would you pay that? If not, what would have to be different?

Question 5 is the one I care about most. Question 6 — please say no if it's no.


6. IF SOMETHING BREAKS
----------------------
Click the PressTalk icon in the menu bar > Settings > "Export Diagnostics".
It saves a text file and opens Finder. Send me that file.

It contains your permission states, your Mac's audio setup, and the app's recent
activity log. It does NOT contain anything you dictated — transcripts are
stripped out before anything is written to disk. You can open it and read it
first; it's plain text.


7. UNINSTALLING
---------------
Drag PressTalk.app to the Trash, then delete:
  ~/Library/Application Support/JarvisTap
  ~/Library/Application Support/FluidAudio
  ~/Library/Logs/presstalk_trace.log
The FluidAudio folder is the model download and is the big one. Nothing else is
left behind, and nothing was ever sent anywhere.


Thanks. Genuinely — tell me if it's bad.
