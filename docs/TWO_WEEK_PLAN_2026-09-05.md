# PressTalk: decision for 5–18 September 2026

**Prepare a paid pilot now; take money after a short installation gate. Cap it at five buyers, $20 each.** Spend on signing now. Stop commercial expansion at the limits below; keep the owner's useful personal tool. Success cannot be guaranteed, but exposure can be bounded.

This supersedes the sequencing and commercial gates in `GO_TO_MARKET.md` and `LAUNCH_GATES.md` for this fortnight. It is a plan, not evidence that its prerequisites have been implemented or passed.

**What changed my decision.** The supplied mbp1 log extraction is instrumented field evidence, not merely a testimonial: 174 recorded dictations on 26 days, with no recorded real failures. The owner's satisfaction and cancelled $15/month subscription are separate self-report and revealed personal value. I have not independently re-extracted the remote log. Logging also cannot by itself prove every word reached the intended field. This supports reliability after setup for one experienced user on 0.1.5; it does not establish fresh-install reliability, independent-user accuracy, or 0.1.7's regression rate.

That is enough to move more laboratory reliability work behind customer exposure and reverse my previous "five promises before $99" ordering. Test the signed product we intend to sell, rather than spend scarce owner time coaching people through an unsigned installation. The old Gate 2 has not passed: I am explicitly replacing its five verbal price acceptances with actual purchases. The fresh-install gate remains mandatory.

**Hard exposure:** three planned owner hours plus **one reserve hour**, never more than **two hours in either week**. All engineering, packaging, test preparation and result collation have a separate **eight-hour engineer/agent allowance** and the $25 incremental-spend cap; this is not hidden owner labour. If the owner must do that engineering personally, it must fit his existing four-hour ceiling or sales slip. No ads or new domain. Keep receipts available for refunds, including after day 14. The membership cost is the first year's spend; payment fees and any unrecovered refund fees are additional.

Apple lists membership at [$99 per year, with regional pricing](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/). Lemon Squeezy has [no ecommerce monthly fee; base transaction fees are 5% + $0.50, with possible extras](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing). Its [store approval typically takes 2–3 business days](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store); neither platform's approval time is guaranteed.

**What must exist before payment.** A Developer ID-signed, notarized and stapled release that passes the actual fresh-install and signed-update tests; an approved Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout; clear compatibility, privacy, support and 14-day refund terms; and a purchase-to-licence-to-offline-unlock rehearsal. The app's trusted licence-key dictionary is currently empty: securely generate/back up the private key and embed its public key before packaging. Disable Lemon Squeezy's separate key generator. Manually fulfil at most five orders within a disclosed two-business-day window. Test fresh trial, simulated day-15 expiry, purchase/unlock, restart and grandfathered access before enabling expiry in the commercial build. Existing free access stays free.

Use a new, identifiable release for this pilot. The supplied v0.1.7 is a pre-release; the cask/latest path does not deliver it. Provide the exact tested ZIP directly. A manual update link is sufficient for five buyers once an actual signed-to-signed replacement preserves permissions. An updater is deferred. Source rebuilding on mbp1 does not pass either installation test.

**Price: retain $20 once; do not discount further.** It already reduces the documented $39 standard price. Keep the existing rights: compatible Macs indefinitely and every future Mac release we make included, with no guaranteed future development or indefinite support. These five places count toward the existing first-25 offer. A lower price anchors perceived value, can recruit people with weaker need, and leaves less money for essentially unchanged support per customer. Five $20 sales produce only $92.50 before tax effects and additional fees. They validate a $20 offer, not demand at $39. Promise bounded asynchronous support, not rapid bespoke fixes.

Selling could reveal the remaining installation unknown too. It would make paying customers the setup experiment and create support/refund obligations before we know onboarding works. Three short unpaid installation checks are cheaper within this owner's time budget. They are the only additional pre-sale field gate, not another month in the lab.

**Quality fallback: off by default, including when its model is cached.** Explicit opt-in remains available. Change runtime and launcher defaults together. The observed 1.220/0.425-second ratio is about 2.9, but those are different utterances, not a randomized estimate of causal cost. More importantly, the documented **3.83 WER-point gain is over the 54 triggered clips, not the full 144**; those clips improved/worsened/tied 18/15/21. Fresh installs already lack the optional model. Re-score the actual default before using the historical 11.25% WER in marketing.

Re-enable by default only after a paired, blinded comparison of **60 natural dictations from three non-owner German speakers** shows at least **10% lower median release-to-finished-corrected-text time for each speaker**, no increase in meaning-changing errors, and **release-to-text p95 ≤1.5 seconds for utterances up to 15 seconds on the slowest supported pilot Mac**. Count every attempt and report failures separately. This evaluation is not a launch prerequisite; absence of evidence keeps the default off. Do not retune a threshold to the owner's 12 samples.

**Retry ladder: fix before the pilot.** Allow at most **one automatic Whisper pass with a shared one-second Whisper wall-clock budget**. Remove automatic relaxed, auto-detect and chunked retries from both finalization paths. On rejection or timeout, insert the already validated primary candidate; never relax recall guards just to return something. Without a valid candidate, end processing visibly and retain the current recording in memory for explicit recovery. A deadline must release the foreground wait, discard late results and prevent overlapping stale inference; merely checking the clock between passes is insufficient. Verify with forced stalls/rejections plus Stage 2's 60 field dictations. Stop release if the one-second budget is exceeded, text is lost/duplicated, or **any of those short field dictations takes over two seconds to deliver**. These are pilot acceptance limits, not a claim that all durations and hardware already meet them.

**The most likely unlisted failure: nobody else needs it often enough to change a habit.** The owner already paid for dictation and knows how to get value from it. Friends downloading, praising or buying out of kindness can hide a product that never becomes part of their work. Recruit existing frequent dictators and count unprompted return plus payment. Downloads alone cannot answer that. If the fortnight fails, stop commercial expansion, preserve buyer rights and honour refunds; do not turn silence into a new feature backlog. If it passes, retain the five-customer cap until their refund windows close and repeat use/support costs remain acceptable.
