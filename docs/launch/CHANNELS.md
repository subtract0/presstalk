# Where the first strangers come from

Researched 2026-09-06 by three models plus seventeen agents, then attacked. Most
of what came back did not survive. What follows is what did, plus the things that
were killed and why — because the killed list is what stops this being redone.

## The reframe that survived everything

Not "a German-first dictation app". The feature is commoditised — Wispr Flow,
Monologue, VoiceInk, Superwhisper, MacParakeet and half a dozen free ones all
dictate on a Mac, and several do German.

> **The only Mac dictation app that publishes its German word error rate — with
> the test set and the scorer, so you can check it.**

The recognition is not the moat. **The evidence is.** Nobody else in this
category publishes a number anyone could falsify, because publishing one means
someone might. We already did the work; we may as well be the only ones who did.

Put that line at the top of every surface: page, forum post, email, listing. And
link the eval set every time, or it is just another claim.

## Order

### 1. Get notarized first. Every channel below converts to zero without it.

Each of these is a one-shot. You get one Show HN, one megathread post, one
first impression. Spending them on a build that shows a malware warning wastes
the shot and you cannot have it back. Apple has the money; wait for the queue.

### 2. r/macapps — **but not the way everyone assumes**

**Rule 1 forbids main-feed self-promotion** unless you qualify through their
trust or transparency requirements. Rule 8 sends everyone else to the **monthly
megathread**. A main-feed "Show" post gets removed, and removal is worse than
silence because it burns the account.

So: post in the megathread, and separately message the moderators about
qualifying. The route to the main feed is weeks of ordinary participation, not a
form. Start the clock now, spend it later.

Cost: 5 minutes of modmail this week, then roughly 20 minutes a week of genuinely
useful commenting. This is the only reliable route to strangers and its lead time
is measured in weeks — which is exactly why it starts before you need it.

### 3. Show HN — high variance, and calibrate expectations honestly

Two direct comparables, both verified:

- *Show HN: Ghost Pepper — Local hold-to-talk speech-to-text for macOS* — 467 points, 200 comments
- *Show HN: Whispering* — 591 points, 152 comments

**Both were free and MIT-licensed.** A $20 paid app in the same slot faces a
harder room, and HN reliably produces a "why not just use whisper.cpp" thread.
Post it as a **measurement**, not as an app:

> Show HN: I measured my Mac dictation app's German error rate against Apple's own engine

That framing survives the "yet another whisper wrapper" reflex, because the
interesting thing genuinely is the measurement. Bring the eval set.

### 4. The German angle — a post, not a repositioning

Do not build a German-only site. Write one thing:

> **Warum Apples deutsche Diktierfunktion scheitert — und was ich dagegen gemacht habe**

with the 19.38% vs 12.71% table and the method. Post it where German Mac users
already are. It builds credibility for the *engine*, which sells the app to
everyone, rather than narrowing the audience to one language.

## Killed, and why — do not resurrect without reading this

**Product Hunt as a launch.** A security warning on first launch destroys
conversion there, and the effort is a full day. Permitted: a 40-minute backlink
post with no hunter outreach and no expectations.

**YouTube outreach.** Cold-pitching creators for a $20 utility with no audience
behind it does not convert. If a German Mac YouTuber ever emails first, send a
free licence within the hour — but never initiate.

**Paid ads.** A $20 one-time product cannot pay for acquisition.

**X/Twitter as a channel.** No following. Post it, but do not count it.

## The honest bottom line

**This is where the whole thing dies, and it is worth saying plainly.**

Every channel that produces a stranger is either gated behind notarization, or
behind weeks of community standing, or was killed above. What is left today is
ten people Alex already knows, and the pilot deliberately closes at five sales.
So the counters — Homebrew installs, GitHub downloads, HN mentions, repo traffic
— will all read zero for a while, and **a zero from no audience looks exactly
like a zero from a broken counter.**

Do not read that as failure. Read it as: the top of the funnel does not exist
yet, and building more product will not create one.
