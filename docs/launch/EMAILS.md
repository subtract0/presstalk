# The emails

Four of them. Each has one job, and only the licence email asks a question —
because it is the only one people reliably answer.

---

## 1. Tester invitation

> Subject: **Would you break my dictation app?**
>
> I built a Mac dictation app. Hold Fn, talk, let go, the text appears where you
> were typing. Runs on your machine, nothing uploaded.
>
> I need people who already dictate to try it on a Mac that has never seen it, and
> tell me where it falls over. Twenty minutes to set up, then use it for real for a
> couple of days.
>
> [link]
>
> Apple Silicon only, macOS 14 or later. It'll be $20 once when it goes on sale, no
> subscription. You'd get it free for testing.
>
> One warning: Apple hasn't finished checking it, so macOS shows a scary-looking
> notice on first launch. Instructions are in the download.

---

## 2. Consent, before any follow-up

Unchecked by default. Never assume it.

> I'd like to email you twice: once about setup, and once after a week to ask how
> it went. That's it — no list, no newsletter, and it doesn't affect your licence.
> Reply "stop" whenever and I'll stop.
>
> The app itself sends me nothing. I have no way of knowing whether you used it,
> which is exactly why I'm asking.

---

## 3. Day seven

> Subject: **Did it earn a place on your Mac?**
>
> I genuinely can't see whether you used it — there's no telemetry in the app, on
> purpose. So I have to ask.
>
> Roughly how many days did you use it this week: 0, 1, 2, or 3+?
>
> If you stopped, what got in the way — the setup, the corrections, forgetting the
> key was there, or you just preferred what you had?
>
> One line is plenty. Please don't send me anything you dictated.

**Do not** send daily reminders to the people you are asking about unprompted
return. Prompting them destroys the only signal you wanted.

---

## 4. The licence, after a sale

The single highest-yield question you will ever ask, and it rides along with
something they already want to open.

> Subject: **Your PressTalk licence**
>
> Thank you. Here's your key:
>
> `PRESSTALK-1.founder-2026.…`
>
> Paste it into Settings → Enter Licence Key. It's checked on your Mac; nothing is
> sent anywhere, and it keeps working if my server, my website and I all vanish.
>
> One question, if you have ten seconds: **what were you using before, and who or
> what pointed you at this?** I have no analytics, so this is genuinely the only
> way I find out.
>
> Fourteen days, full refund, no argument.

Log the answers in a plain file next to the metrics snapshots. At this scale it
is testimony, not measurement — and the pilot's target is testimony-sized, so the
instrument fits the question.

---

## Day thirty

> Still using it? Roughly how many days a week?

That is the whole email. Short questions get answered.
