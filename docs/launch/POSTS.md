## Answers when someone asks

**“Does it make mistakes?”**

> It can. The useful test is a sentence you would actually write, on your own Mac.
> The trial lets you do that before paying. If you want measurement details, the
> reproducible German evaluation and its limitations are available here:
> [verified evidence URL].

**“Why would I use it?”**

> To hold one key, say what you want to write, and have the text appear where
> you're already working. Try it with a real reply and see whether that feels
> useful to you.

**“How do you know what users do without analytics?”**

> I don't. The app sends no usage data. I learn from people who choose to tell
> me what worked or where they got stuck.

## What was removed

The old openings about published error rates, another product's mistakes,
comparison pricing and engineering victories are retired. Do not revive them
for Hacker News or use a provocative competitor headline to rescue a quiet launch.
Evidence remains available for a doubter. The introduction shows a person finishing
a sentence.
